# Content rating (IARC questionnaire)

Play Console > App content > Content rating. Email address: your contact address.

**Category:** All other app types (utility / productivity).

| Question | Answer |
|---|---|
| Violence (any kind) | No |
| Blood or gore | No |
| Sexual content or nudity | No |
| Profanity or crude humour | No |
| Reference to or depiction of drugs, alcohol or tobacco | No |
| Gambling or simulated gambling | No |
| Horror or fear themes | No |
| Does the app let users communicate or share content with other users? | No |
| Does the app share the user's location with other users? | No |
| Can users purchase digital goods inside the app? | No |
| Does the app provide unrestricted web access? | No (it opens a saved link in the user's own browser) |
| Does the app contain user-generated content visible to others? | No (everything stays on the device) |

**Expected result:** the lowest rating in every region (for example Everyone / PEGI 3 / USK 0).
The Console shows the final result after you submit.
