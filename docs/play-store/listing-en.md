## App name

ExpiryTicker: Renewal Tracker

## Short description

Never miss an expiry. Reminders for insurance, licences, warranties, renewals.

## Full description

Never pay for a late renewal or find an expired document again. ExpiryTicker keeps every expiry date and renewal in one place and reminds you before each one is due.

Add insurance, passports, driving licences, warranties, subscriptions, domains, memberships, certifications and anything else with a date. See at a glance what is overdue, due today, or coming up, and get a notification at the time you choose.

REMINDERS YOU CAN TRUST
• Reminders before the due date and again on the day, plus extra ones such as 30, 14 or 3 days ahead
• Choose the time of day reminders arrive
• Mark an item done, or snooze it until tomorrow, straight from the notification
• Reminders are re-planned if your time zone or clock changes

SEE WHAT NEEDS ATTENTION
• A clear "next up" card and items grouped as overdue, today, next 7 days, next 30 days and later
• Colour-coded urgency with a large countdown on every item
• A home-screen widget with a one-tap Done button
• Swipe to complete or delete, with Undo
• Search across titles, notes and categories, in any word order

RENEW WITH LESS EFFORT
• Save the renewal website and the provider's phone number, then open them in one tap
• Add the due date to your calendar
• Attach the policy, receipt or certificate as a photo, PDF or image
• Recurring items renew to their next date automatically when you mark them done

KEEP TRACK OF COSTS
• Record what you paid at each renewal and see how prices change over time
• Insights show your yearly cost, the next 12 months, spending by category and price increases
• Choose your currency from 12 options

PRIVATE BY DESIGN
• No account, no ads, no tracking
• Your items stay on your device
• Optional app lock with fingerprint, face or your screen lock
• Back up everything to a single file and restore it any time, or export a CSV spreadsheet

MADE TO FEEL AT HOME
• Light and dark themes
• English and Hindi

ExpiryTicker is a simple, calm way to stay on top of the dates that matter.
