# ExpiryTicker Privacy Policy

**Effective date:** 27 November 2026

ExpiryTicker ("the app") helps you keep track of things that expire or renew and reminds you before they are due. This policy explains what the app does with your information. In short: **the app keeps your data on your device, and the developer does not collect it.**

## What the app stores

Everything you enter is stored only on your device, in the app's private storage:

- the items you add: title, category, due date, reminder settings, repeat schedule, expected cost, notes, website address and phone number;
- the history of renewals you record, including the amounts you enter;
- files and photos you choose to attach to an item;
- your settings (theme, currency, reminder time, app lock on or off).

The app has no account, no sign-in and no server. It does not ask for the internet permission, so it cannot send this information anywhere by itself. The developer does not receive, see or store any of it.

## What the app does not do

- It does not contain advertising, analytics, tracking or crash-reporting libraries.
- It does not collect your location, contacts, microphone, or advertising ID.
- It does not sell or share your information with anyone.

## Permissions and system features

- **Notifications.** Used to show the reminders you set. You can switch them off in Android's settings; the app will then tell you reminders are off.
- **Biometrics / screen lock (optional).** If you turn on App lock, the app asks Android to verify you with your fingerprint, face or device PIN. Android performs the check; the app never receives your biometric data or PIN.
- **Camera and files.** "Take photo" opens your camera app, and "Attach file" opens Android's file picker. The app receives only the photo or file you pick, and stores a copy in its private storage.
- **Reminders in the background.** The app schedules short background tasks so reminders arrive on time, and re-schedules them after a restart or a change of time or time zone.

## Other apps and services involved

Some actions hand your information to another app, which then follows its own privacy policy:

- **Website** opens the address you saved in your browser. **Call** opens your phone app with the number you saved. **Add to calendar** opens your calendar app with the item's title, category, notes and date. Opening an attachment uses whichever app you choose to view it.
- **Typeface.** The app's text uses the Inter typeface, which Google Play services may download from Google Fonts. This is a standard Android mechanism run by Google Play services; the app itself sends nothing.
- **Android backup.** If you have turned on Android's device backup, Android may copy the app's data (items, history and settings, but not attachments) to your Google account, under Google's terms. You can turn this off in your device's backup settings.
- **Files you export.** "Export to CSV" and "Back up everything" create a file in a place you choose. Those files contain your items and, for the backup, your attachments. They are not encrypted, and what happens to them afterwards is up to you, so store or share them carefully.
- **Widget and notifications.** The home-screen widget and reminders show your item titles. If your phone's lock screen shows notifications or widgets, others may see those titles. You can change this in Android's settings.

## Keeping your data safe

Your data lives in the app's private storage, which other apps cannot read on a normal, unrooted device. You can add App lock for an extra layer. No data is sent over a network by the app.

## Retention and deletion

Your data stays on your device until you remove it. You can delete any item inside the app, and uninstalling the app removes everything it stored. Copies you exported, and any Android device backup, are not deleted by uninstalling; remove those yourself if you no longer want them.

## Children

The app is a general-purpose utility and is not directed at children under 13. The developer does not knowingly collect information from anyone.

## Your rights

Because the developer holds no personal data about you, there is nothing for the developer to access, correct, export or delete on your behalf. Everything is in the app on your device, under your control.
